Welcome to PIPS • Est. 2009

Not just a college.
A home for future pharmacists.

Established in 2009 as a graduate institute dedicated to the health professions, PIPS is an exciting and dynamic place to visit and learn. We pride ourselves on a learner-centered academic and professional environment — preparing graduates for success in their chosen health-related fields.

At the heart of our philosophy is a belief in the transformative power of education: skills, knowledge and values to thrive in a dynamic, ever-evolving world — with distinguished faculty, innovative programs and state-of-the-art facilities.

Tablets

Precision pressed into every dose. In Pharmaceutics you learn how powders become tablets — compression, coating, disintegration and dissolution — the most trusted dosage form in the world.

02 / 03 — PHARMACEUTICS

Capsules

A shell with a secret. Hard and soft gelatin capsules protect sensitive medicines and release them exactly where they are needed. At PIPS, you open, fill, seal — and understand — the science inside.

Hard & Soft Gelatin • Targeted Release

Program — Pharm-D

Doctor of Pharmacy.

Pharm-D is an undergraduate five-year degree program for those interested in pursuing a career in health and Pharmaceutical Sciences. Students with a medical background can opt for this degree, fulfilling the admission criteria.

During their stay at PIPS, students study various subjects and go through practical training — including hospital internships for professional practice in real hospital settings — to qualify for the degree of Doctor of Pharmacy — recognized by the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan and widely accepted nationally and internationally. Graduates fill the increasing demand for retail, hospital and industrial pharmacists, and can join government and research organizations.
